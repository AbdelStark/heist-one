# Code of conduct

HEIST//ONE should be a place where people can challenge technical ideas without
making participation hostile or unsafe.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct about code, evidence, and tradeoffs while remaining respectful to
  the people doing the work.
- Welcome questions and differing experience levels.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and separate measured results from opinion.
- Respect privacy. Never repost credentials, private traces, or personal data.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and sustained personal attacks are not tolerated.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and public
spaces used to represent the project. Maintainers may edit or remove content,
close contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
behavior violates this policy.

For a confidential conduct report, use the repository's private
[reporting form](https://github.com/AbdelStark/heist-one/security/advisories/new)
and begin the title with `[conduct]`. Include links or screenshots and any
context needed to assess the report. Reports will be handled as privately as the
investigation allows.
